# Founder video workflow offer

Use when Chris/Roger are shaping the first tangible offer around personalized agents with connected “súper poderes” for AI video creation.

## Positioning

Do not sell this as a generic chatbot. Sell it as an **agente personalizado con un súper poder de video IA**: a personalized assistant plus an assisted workflow that helps turn ideas, scripts, documents, or brand context into video assets.

Core phrase:

> “Te instalamos un agente con súper poder de video IA para que puedas convertir ideas, guiones o documentos en contenido usando herramientas como HeyGen, Drive y B-roll con IA. No solo te damos el sistema: también te acompañamos para que aprendas a usarlo y empieces a producir.”

## Commercial ladder

1. **48-hour free trial — Gratis**
   - Personalized demo.
   - Limited access.
   - Basic business/context personalization.
   - One sample use case or workflow preview.
   - No permanent installation or heavy support.

2. **First superpower activation — $2,000 MXN**
   - Install/configure one assisted AI video workflow.
   - Suggested stack: Google Drive + HeyGen avatar + AI B-roll tool such as Higgsfield + Premiere Pro or editing handoff.
   - Include folder/template structure, base prompts, functional test, and mini usage guide.
   - Position as **workflow asistido**, not fully autonomous end-to-end automation.

3. **Community accompaniment — $2,000 MXN**
   - Group support/community.
   - Tutorials, templates, prompts, examples, feedback, and Q&A.
   - Clarify per offer whether this is a one-time cohort, a fixed-period access, or a monthly/lifetime founder deal.

## Suggested packages

- **Solo instalación:** $2,000 MXN.
- **Instalación + comunidad:** $4,000 MXN.
- **Founder option:** $3,500 MXN for first clients in exchange for feedback, testimonial, and permission to use an anonymized case study.

## Affiliate-program utility

Tool affiliate programs can improve margin, but they are not the main business model. Use them as complementary revenue when clients need accounts for tools in the workflow.

Confirmed example from HeyGen affiliate page:

- HeyGen affiliate program exists.
- 35% commission.
- Recurring for the first 3 months of each referred customer.
- Applies to Creator and Team plans.
- 30-day attribution window, last click.
- Monthly payouts via PayPal.
- Minimum payout: $30 USD.
- Commissions become eligible after a 60-day verification period.
- Official link: https://www.heygen.com/affiliate-program

How to frame it internally:

1. Direct revenue: $2,000 MXN workflow activation.
2. Expansion revenue: $2,000 MXN community/accompaniment.
3. Complementary revenue: affiliate commissions from tools like HeyGen when clients subscribe through the recommended link.

## Scope guardrails

For the $2,000 MXN activation, do **not** include:

- Unlimited videos.
- Custom editing or unlimited revisions.
- Paying the client’s external tool subscriptions.
- Complex API/MCP/CLI integrations unless quoted separately.
- Guaranteeing viral results or specific business outcomes.
- Full autonomous publishing/editing unless separately scoped.

Client responsibilities:

- Provide business information, scripts, documents, or source material.
- Create/pay their own tool accounts.
- Approve scripts and final content.
- Use the workflow and participate in community if purchased.

## Flyer design note

For client-facing flyers about this offer, keep copy large and readable. A proven simple structure:

- Header: “Agente con Súper Poder de Video IA”.
- Subheader: “Workflow asistido para crear videos con IA”.
- Single-column cards:
  1. Prueba 48 hrs — GRATIS.
  2. Activación — $2,000 MXN.
  3. Comunidad — $2,000 MXN.
- Highlight: “Instalación + comunidad: $4,000 MXN”.
- Small but readable affiliate note: “Extra: afiliados de herramientas. Ejemplo: HeyGen paga 35% por 3 meses.”

Avoid tiny legal text, crowded two-column cards, and any text that risks clipping on Telegram/WhatsApp mobile previews.
