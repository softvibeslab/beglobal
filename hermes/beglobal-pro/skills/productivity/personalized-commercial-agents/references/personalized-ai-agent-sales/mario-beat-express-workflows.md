# Mario / BEAT EXPRESS workflows

Use this reference when helping Mario Villanueva sell BEAT EXPRESS or present the Mario personalized assistant.

## Current assets and constraints

- BEAT EXPRESS is a low-ticket product priced at **$27 USD**.
- Landing page exists at `https://delseralvender.com` and is connected to Hotmart checkout.
- Landing headline observed: “Vende Mejor en 60 Minutos”.
- Core promise: short course to order the way people sell, recover confidence, and close without pressure using BEAT.
- Mario’s Instagram profile positions him as “Speaker & Coach de ventas”. Bio signals: “Ventas con ritmo”, “BEAT: simple, directo y real”, “Escribe BEAT para info.”
- Instagram signals observed: ~11.4k followers, ~76.2k views in last 30 days, content with high-view outliers around 47.5k and 75.4k views.

## Mario’s stated priorities

1. Grow personal brand.
2. Increase sales of BEAT EXPRESS.
3. Start shaping an app project for sellers.
4. Grow audience and views.
5. Reduce time spent finding clients and creating content.

Primary success criterion:

> Increase sales of BEAT EXPRESS.

## Recommended sequence

Do not start from “build product” because product, landing, and payment path already exist. Start closest to money:

1. **Audit landing conversion**
   - clarity of first screen
   - audience specificity
   - pain specificity
   - CTA clarity
   - objections/FAQ
   - trust proof/testimonials
   - BEAT EXPRESS → BEAT PRO ladder

2. **Build traffic workflow**
   - Reels/posts/stories that point to BEAT EXPRESS
   - CTAs like “Escribe BEAT”, “Comenta BEAT”, “Te mando acceso”
   - Repurpose winning posts into multiple formats

3. **Build DM conversion workflow**
   - reply for “BEAT” keyword
   - qualify lightly
   - send landing naturally
   - follow-up sequence for non-buyers
   - objections: price, time, “no soy vendedor”, “vendo por WhatsApp/Instagram”

4. **Build content workflow**
   - hooks from real pains: “te piden precio y desaparecen”, “te dejan en visto”, “sientes que persigues clientes”
   - short scripts for Reels
   - carousel/story prompts
   - CTA mapped to landing

5. **Build measurement workflow**
   - track which content gets views/clicks/DMs
   - identify themes from high-performing posts
   - keep a simple list of leads, DM status, and purchases when available

6. **Seller-app validation workflow**
   - after BEAT EXPRESS sales motion is working, validate the seller app with audience and buyers
   - define user, pain, feature set, willingness to pay, and early waitlist offer

## Presenting the assistant to Mario

Tone: human, not vendor-like. Avoid saying “mini interview” or giving a fixed time. Start with the difference from ChatGPT, then offer two paths.

Suggested framing:

> Mario, algo importante: esto no es lo mismo que abrir ChatGPT y hacer preguntas sueltas. ChatGPT es una herramienta general. Yo estoy pensado como tu asistente personalizado: un aliado que puede trabajar con tu contexto, tu voz, tus objetivos, tus proyectos, tus relaciones y la forma en la que quieres moverte.
>
> Puedo hacerte unas preguntas para adaptar mejor los recursos a ti, o si ya tienes algo en mente —una idea, problema, oportunidad o proyecto— podemos empezar por ahí.

Mention that with Roger/Chris authorization the assistant can gain more “súper poderes”: documents, tools, workflows, automations, systems, and knowledge sources.

## Style notes for this user/context

- Use casual, friendly Spanish without forced slang.
- Do not use “bro”.
- Prefer “en resumen” over “en corto”.
- Keep outputs short, practical, and iterative.
