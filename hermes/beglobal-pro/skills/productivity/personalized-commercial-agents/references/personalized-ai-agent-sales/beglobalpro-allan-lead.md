# Be Global Pro / Allan Gerardo lead pattern

Use this reference for prospects similar to `@beglobalpro` and `@allangerardomx`: ecommerce educators, franchise/community models, personal-brand founders with large Instagram audiences, course libraries, and high-volume DM lead capture.

## Public signals observed

### Be Global Pro

- Instagram positioning: “Tu aliado ideal para vender por internet.”
- Public follower/post scale observed: ~36K followers, ~1.6K posts.
- Bio themes: “Franquicia Digital”, “modelo probado sin inventario”, “formación empresarial real”, “+10,000 socios en la comunidad”.
- Website positioning: start a dropshipping/ecommerce store without inventory; Be Global Pro manages inventory and gives access to international suppliers.
- Offers/assets: product catalog, memberships, store, calendar, courses, community, mentoring, ecommerce education.
- Course topics observed: Shopify, Amazon, Mercado Libre, Facebook Ads, Google Ads, TikTok, funnels, copywriting, content, customer service, AI, product research, pricing, UGC, TikTok Shop, importing from China, certifications.

### Allan Gerardo

- Instagram positioning: “Ecommerce con Allan Gerardo.”
- Public follower/post scale observed: ~346K followers, ~1.8K posts.
- Bio CTA: DM keyword “NEGOCIO” to learn how to start a real ecommerce business.
- Founder of Be Global Pro; podcast host.
- Website positioning: mentor in ecommerce and digital businesses with purpose; helps entrepreneurs build real systems that generate income and freedom.
- Brand narrative: personal transformation, faith/family/purpose, ecommerce, education, community, legacy.

## Business read

This is not a simple chatbot lead. Treat it as a **content + sales + community scaling** opportunity:

> They already have audience, product, community, authority, and a repeatable model. The opportunity is to convert better every DM, educate leads before sales calls, standardize commercial answers, support members, and scale Allan/Be Global Pro’s voice without making the founder or team repeat the same explanations.

## Likely pains

- High-volume DMs from keyword CTAs like “NEGOCIO”.
- Repeated questions about how the model works, cost, experience needed, inventory, logistics, providers, support, and expected results.
- Need to filter serious leads from curious followers.
- Leads going cold because follow-up is inconsistent.
- Founder/creator becoming the bottleneck for responses, ideas, and trust.
- Large member/community support burden.
- Course library can feel overwhelming unless members know what to take next.
- Sales team may need consistent answers, scripts, and objection handling.

## Strongest agent use cases

### 1. Lead qualification agent

For people who DM “NEGOCIO” or ask how to start.

Capabilities:
- Explain the model simply.
- Ask qualifying questions: goal, experience, budget, timeline, current job/business, urgency.
- Classify lead: curious / early learner / ready for advisor / potential franchise/member.
- Recommend next step: video, call, advisor, course, community, event.
- Produce a summary for the sales team.

### 2. Objection-handling commercial agent

Train on objections like:
- “No tengo experiencia.”
- “No tengo dinero.”
- “¿Esto sí funciona?”
- “¿Es dropshipping?”
- “¿Necesito inventario?”
- “¿Cuánto tiempo tarda en vender?”
- “¿Qué incluye la franquicia?”
- “¿Me acompañan?”
- “¿Es seguro?”

Goal: consistent, human, non-hype answers in Allan/Be Global Pro style.

### 3. Content copilot for Allan

Turn ideas into:
- Reels, carousels, captions, hooks, stories, podcast outlines, live scripts, launch emails, DM CTAs.
- Repurpose content around ecommerce, purpose, discipline, systems, faith/family/legacy, and business education.

### 4. Community/member support agent

For Be Global Pro members/socios:
- Guide which course to take next.
- Answer operational questions about ecommerce, products, ads, customer service, platforms.
- Help members respond to customers and choose products.
- Reduce repetitive support and improve member outcomes.

### 5. Sales-team training agent

For advisors/closers:
- Roleplay calls and DMs.
- Practice objections.
- Review conversations.
- Generate follow-ups and scripts.
- Standardize the commercial message.

## When user brings rough meeting notes / handwritten maps

If Roger shares blurry handwritten notes or says he had a meeting with Allan/Be Global, do not over-focus on exact transcription. Treat the page as a visual scaffold and immediately connect it to the known Be Global opportunity:

- Allan + wife / Be Global likely means an ecommerce education/franchise/community ecosystem, not only a single dropshipping store.
- Read the map through the lenses of: product/niche, content, ads, WhatsApp/DM capture, agent, community support, and sales workflow.
- Acknowledge uncertainty in the handwriting briefly, then move to the strategic synthesis: “esto parece un sistema comercial de ecommerce con IA.”
- Ask for the meeting context next, but offer to organize it into: what was discussed, real opportunity, value proposition, phases, roles, first proposal, and pricing/packages.
- Keep the Spanish casual and iterative; avoid making the user re-explain old Be Global context if this reference already covers it.

## Meeting angle

Open with business, not technology:

> “Allan ya tiene algo muy valioso: audiencia, autoridad y una comunidad que confía. Lo que vemos es una oportunidad para que la IA no reemplace su voz, sino que la escale. Un agente personalizado podría ayudarles a responder mejor leads, filtrar prospectos, entrenar socios, crear contenido y acompañar mejor a la comunidad sin que Allan o el equipo tengan que repetir lo mismo mil veces.”

Core positioning:

> “No queremos ponerles un bot genérico. Queremos construir un agente entrenado con Be Global Pro: su modelo, sus cursos, sus respuestas, sus objeciones, sus productos y el tono de Allan, para que les ayude a convertir más leads, acompañar mejor a su comunidad y liberar tiempo del equipo.”

## Demo to prepare

Show 3 concrete mini demos:

1. **Lead DM**
   - User: “Hola, vi que puedo empezar un ecommerce sin inventario. ¿Cómo funciona?”
   - Agent: explains simply, asks 3 qualifying questions, suggests next step.

2. **Objection**
   - User: “Me interesa, pero no tengo experiencia y me da miedo invertir.”
   - Agent: empathetic response, explains accompaniment, reduces risk, invites next step.

3. **Content**
   - Prompt: “Crea un Reel estilo Allan para explicar por qué vender por internet no se trata de suerte, sino de sistema.”
   - Agent outputs hook, script, caption, and CTA to DM “NEGOCIO”.

## Diagnostic questions for the call

Ask these before proposing implementation:

1. ¿De dónde les llegan más leads hoy: Instagram, WhatsApp, web, eventos o comunidad?
2. Cuando alguien manda “NEGOCIO”, ¿qué proceso sigue después?
3. ¿Cuáles son las 5 preguntas que más les hacen antes de comprar o entrar?
4. ¿Qué parte les consume más tiempo: responder prospectos, dar seguimiento, crear contenido o apoyar socios?
5. ¿Tienen equipo comercial o Allan/Dulce siguen muy involucrados en el cierre?
6. ¿Qué información les gustaría que todo prospecto entendiera antes de hablar con un asesor?
7. Si un agente pudiera ahorrarles 10 horas a la semana, ¿dónde tendría más impacto?

## Offer sequencing

Recommend phased selling:

1. **Agente Comercial Be Global Pro** — leads, FAQs, objections, follow-ups, scripts, sales summaries.
2. **Agente de Comunidad/Socios** — support, course guidance, ecommerce help, member enablement.
3. **Súper poderes** — CRM, WhatsApp, calendar, forms, reports, knowledge base, automations.

Scope guardrail:

> “Primero construimos el cerebro del agente con su contexto. Después, si hace sentido, le agregamos súper poderes para conectarlo a sus herramientas.”

Avoid promising fully autonomous sales, universal platform integrations, or closing without humans before discovery.
