# Founder Personalized Agent Offer

Use this reference when Chris/Roger need a simple plan to present or sell personalized intelligent agents without overcomplicating tiers.

## Positioning

Do not sell “chatbots.” Sell a personalized intelligent agent/copilot that understands the client’s business, tone, customers, processes, services, objections, and goals.

Core promise:

> “Te construimos un agente inteligente personalizado con tu contexto, tu tono y tus procesos para que puedas crear contenido, responder mejor, ordenar ideas, vender y trabajar más rápido. No es un chatbot genérico: es tu copiloto personalizado.”

Short phrase:

> “Construye tu agente inteligente personalizado para ahorrar tiempo, ordenar tu negocio y maximizar tu workflow.”

## Offer ladder

### 1. Free 48-hour trial

Price: **Gratis**

Includes:
- personalized demo
- basic agent with prospect context
- 1 applied use case from their business
- quick sample of how the agent would help

Goal: make the prospect say “sí lo quiero para mí.”

Do not include permanent installation, heavy support, advanced automations, or unlimited use.

### 2. Agent activation

Price: **$2,000 MXN**

Includes:
- initial agent configuration
- business context load
- communication tone
- FAQs
- offers/services
- base prompts
- quick-use guide
- installation/setup of tools needed to maximize the client’s workflow

### 3. Accompaniment / Done With You / community

Price: **$2,000 MXN**

Includes:
- group accompaniment or guided implementation
- sessions/tutorials
- prompts
- templates
- examples
- doubt solving
- agent improvement ideas
- use cases for sales, content, and operations

Use **“Done With You / hecho contigo”** when the offer should feel more premium than “comunidad.”

## Recommended package

**Agent + accompaniment: $4,000 MXN**

Suggested name:

> Agente Inteligente Personalizado — Done With You

## Initial ideal customers

Start with people who already have a business but are saturated and decide fast:
- coaches
- consultants
- content creators
- experts selling services
- small agencies
- independent salespeople
- business owners with many messages
- infoproduct creators
- people already using ChatGPT but without a system

Do not start with large enterprise unless a warm opportunity exists; validate first with fast-moving buyers.

## Pains to hit

- “Tengo muchas ideas pero no las ordeno.”
- “Pierdo tiempo explicando lo mismo.”
- “No doy seguimiento.”
- “Me cuesta crear contenido.”
- “No tengo procesos documentados.”
- “Uso ChatGPT pero siempre tengo que explicarle todo.”
- “Quiero usar IA pero no sé cómo aterrizarla a mi negocio.”

## Sales process

1. Source leads through Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, current contacts, referrals, Mario/Roger/Chris community, and people already selling services.
2. Offer the 48-hour free trial.
3. Mini diagnostic with 5 questions:
   - ¿Qué vendes?
   - ¿Quién es tu cliente ideal?
   - ¿Qué tareas repites mucho?
   - ¿Dónde pierdes más tiempo?
   - ¿Qué te gustaría que un agente hiciera por ti?
4. Build demo with 1–3 concrete uses: prospect responses, content, idea organization, follow-ups, proposals, scripts, process documentation.
5. Close with:

> “Si te hizo sentido, podemos activarte tu agente completo por $2,000 MXN. Y si quieres que lo hagamos contigo paso a paso, está el acompañamiento por otros $2,000 MXN.”

## Content strategy for the educational/demo channel

Start educational + demonstration because most people do not yet understand what an intelligent agent is.

Content pillars:
1. Basic education: what is an intelligent agent, what it is not, agent vs ChatGPT vs chatbot, why context matters.
2. Simple demos: agent for prospect replies, content creation, idea organization, scripts, follow-ups, proposals, FAQs.
3. Niche use cases: coaches, consultants, salespeople, creators, agencies, local services.
4. Before/after: from explaining everything to ChatGPT every time → agent already knows the business.
5. Soft offer: “Escríbeme AGENTE y te hacemos una prueba gratis de 48 horas.”

First video ideas:
- “ChatGPT no conoce tu negocio. Un agente personalizado sí.”
- “No necesitas más prompts, necesitas contexto.”
- “Le dimos contexto de un coach a un agente y mira cómo responde.”
- “3 tareas que un agente puede quitarte esta semana.”
- “Cómo funciona la prueba gratis de 48 horas.”

Empecemos vendiendo agentes inteligentes personalizados con prueba gratis de 48 horas, activación de $2,000 pesos y acompañamiento de $2,000 pesos. Validamos los primeros 10 clientes, sacamos testimonios y después subimos el ticket agregando súper poderes, workflows y mensualidades.”
