# Telegram offer-channel → AI marketplace strategy

Use this reference when helping Mario/Softvibes pitch owners of large Telegram offer/coupon/affiliate channels, especially channels that monetize via Amazon/Mercado Libre links.

## Prospect pattern

Signals:
- Large buyer audience (e.g. 100K+ subscribers).
- High post/link volume.
- Monetization through affiliate links, coupons, promos, or brand placements.
- Public channel with limited two-way interaction.
- Likely pains: manual curation, weak segmentation, limited buyer intent data, difficulty proving value to sponsors, lost questions/requests, low personalization.

## Positioning

Do **not** pitch “put a bot in your channel.” Pitch:

> “Convert the attention you already have into a smarter, more measurable commerce system.”

Core promise:

> “You already have the audience. We can help you increase the value of every offer by adding an AI layer that recommends, categorizes, answers, measures intent, and creates new monetization paths.”

## Strategic offer

Start with a low-risk pilot, not control of the channel:

1. Separate Telegram bot or mini-agent, not inside the main channel at first.
2. Train it on a sample of public offers/products/categories.
3. Let users ask for products: “laptop under $10k,” “top offers today,” “products to resell,” etc.
4. Track queries, categories, clicks, and buyer intent.
5. Use data to propose deeper integration, VIP community, web marketplace, or sponsorship packages.

## AI marketplace use cases

- Top offers of the day/week.
- Category discovery: tech, home, fashion, beauty, kids, automotive, etc.
- Product comparison and recommendation.
- “Products to resell” lists with margin/copy ideas.
- Buyer FAQ assistant.
- Offer copy optimizer.
- Affiliate-link organizer.
- Sponsor/media-kit generator.
- Brand proposal generator.
- Simple performance report: clicks, questions, categories requested, top products.

## Outreach structure

1. Acknowledge the asset: “tienen comunidad y atención compradora.”
2. Name the opportunity: “monetizar mejor, medir mejor, segmentar mejor.”
3. Offer a pilot: “14 días, bot separado, sin tocar operación principal.”
4. Show concrete use cases: recommendations, top offers, product search, sponsor reports.
5. CTA: “¿Quién ve colaboraciones/crecimiento para mostrarles una mini demo?”

## Pitfalls

- Don’t ask for admin access or control of the channel upfront.
- Don’t over-focus on automation; lead with monetization and measurement.
- Don’t imply guaranteed affiliate revenue or guaranteed conversion lift.
- Don’t pitch too technically; show the marketplace vision in business language.
- Don’t make the channel owner feel replaced; position the agent as an added commerce layer.
