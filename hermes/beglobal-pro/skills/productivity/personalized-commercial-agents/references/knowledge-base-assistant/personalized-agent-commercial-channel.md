# Personalized agent commercial channel

Use this reference when a user wants to package a KB/persona agent as a sellable service, especially for coaches, creators, sales teams, founders, and personal brands.

## Core positioning

Do not sell “a chatbot.” Sell a **personalized AI copilot** trained on the client’s voice, brand, offers, processes, goals, FAQs, objections, and operating context.

Short promise:

> We create an AI agent trained with your brand, voice, processes, and goals so you can save time, sell better, create content, and execute faster.

Even shorter:

> A copilot that knows your business and works as an extension of your head.

## Primary client profiles

1. **Personal brand / creator / coach**
   - Needs content, prospect replies, follow-ups, offers, and idea organization.
2. **Sales team / service business**
   - Needs consistent answers, objection handling, proposals, follow-up, and knowledge transfer.
3. **Busy business owner**
   - Needs clarity, summaries, priorities, meeting prep, and decision support.

## Offer components

A practical base package can include:

- Business/brand/objective diagnosis.
- Initial knowledge base.
- Tone/personality rules.
- Sales/content/operations use cases.
- FAQ, objection, and follow-up flows.
- Telegram or chosen-channel integration.
- Guided demo with client-specific examples.
- Iterative tone/behavior adjustments.

## Differentiator language

Most people sell automation. This offer sells judgment + context + tools.

Use phrases like:

- “not a generic chatbot”
- “trained with your real context”
- “your voice, your services, your clients, your objections”
- “helps you think, create, decide, and execute faster”
- “works with you, not instead of you”

Avoid overpromising revenue, guaranteed outcomes, or autonomous decisions without human review.

## Sales-call structure using BEAT-like flow

1. **Trust** — understand the business; do not pitch AI as a trend.
2. **Need** — ask where time is lost, which answers repeat, which follow-ups drop, where content/ops bottleneck.
3. **Help** — show 2–3 concrete use cases using the prospect’s own context.
4. **Hurry** — close on a demo/prototype/setup next step.

Useful questions:

- Where do you lose the most time right now?
- What do you explain over and over?
- What would the agent need to do for you to say “this actually helps”?
- Which area matters first: sales, content, operations, or decisions?

## Launch CTA

> ¿Quieres ver cómo se vería un agente entrenado con tu negocio? Mándame la palabra AGENTE y te hago una demo rápida.

Alternatives: COPILOTO, DEMO, AI.

## Content angles

- “No necesitas otro chatbot. Necesitas un copiloto que conozca tu negocio.”
- “La AI genérica responde. Un agente personalizado entiende contexto.”
- “Si repites la misma explicación 20 veces por semana, eso ya debería vivir en tu agente.”

## Pitfalls

- Do not lead with technical features; lead with time saved, clarity, consistency, and faster execution.
- Do not imply the agent replaces the owner or team.
- Do not invent integrations, guarantees, pricing, or case-study results.
- For a first outreach, prefer a short DM and demo invitation over a long proposal document.
