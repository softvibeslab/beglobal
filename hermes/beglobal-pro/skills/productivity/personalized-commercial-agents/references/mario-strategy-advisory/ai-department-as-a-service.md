# AI Department as a Service — Mario/Chris/Roger/Allan

Use this reference when the team is shaping a high-value AI solutions business around Mario, Chris, Roger, and Allan.

## Strategic positioning

Do **not** sell “chatbots” or generic “AI solutions.” Position the offer as a business outcome:

> Convertimos el conocimiento comercial de tu empresa en agentes de IA que entrenan, acompañan y ayudan a tu equipo a vender con más consistencia.

Best umbrella offer:

**Sales Academy AI Department** / **Departamento de IA como Servicio para ventas, capacitación y operación comercial**.

This combines:
- sales strategy and authority (Mario)
- offer, marketing, and closing (Chris)
- AI production, prompts, avatars, and demos (Roger)
- technical architecture, productization, integrations, and scale (Allan)

## What differentiates the team

- Not only technical implementation: strategy + sales + content + agents + execution.
- Mario provides commercial authority and a methodology bridge: Del Ser al Vender + AI applied to sales.
- Chris translates technology into offer, pitch, market, and closing assets.
- Roger makes the promise visible through demos, agents, prompts, videos, and AI workflows.
- Allan should own technical feasibility, architecture, integrations, operational reliability, and scalability.

## Recommended business model

Best model: **setup + monthly retainer + expansion modules**.

1. **Diagnostic / executive assessment**
   - $1,500–$5,000 USD
   - Diagnostic, AI opportunity map, implementation proposal, conceptual demo.

2. **Pilot in 7–14 days**
   - $5,000–$15,000 USD
   - 1–3 functional agents, client knowledge base, initial flows, team training, first indicators.

3. **AI Department implementation**
   - $15,000–$50,000 USD setup
   - Architecture, agents by area, automations/integrations, documentation, training, first operational version.

4. **Monthly retainer**
   - $5,000–$30,000 USD/month
   - Support, optimization, new agents/flows, reporting, training, content or automation improvements.

For a mature mid/enterprise client, the upper ceiling can be **$50,000 USD setup + $30,000 USD/month** if tied to measurable revenue, savings, productivity, or training impact.

For first case studies, use a founder pilot:
- $5,000–$7,500 USD pilot
- then convert to $15,000–$25,000 setup + $5,000–$10,000/month.

## Package ladder

### AI Starter
- $5,000 USD setup + $2,000 USD/month
- 1 agent, 1 knowledge base, 1 commercial flow, basic training, limited support.

### AI Sales Department
- $15,000 USD setup + $7,500 USD/month
- 3–5 agents: trainer, follow-up, objections, content; basic Drive/CRM/WhatsApp integration; commercial training; monthly reports.

### AI Enterprise Department
- $35,000–$50,000 USD setup + $15,000–$30,000 USD/month
- Multiple departments, agents by role, advanced integrations, permissions/security, dashboards, continuous training, priority support, monthly roadmap.

## First 7-day demo

Build a **Sales Academy AI Demo** with 4 agents:
1. **Agente Entrenador** — roleplays, corrects responses, trains reps.
2. **Agente de Objeciones** — adapts arguments and improves scripts.
3. **Agente de Seguimiento** — drafts WhatsApp/CRM follow-up and classifies opportunities.
4. **Agente de Contenido Comercial** — produces scripts, reels, emails, presentations, and sales copy.

The demo should show business value in 15 minutes and be customizable in 48–72 hours.

## Key strategic caution

Do not lead with the newest tools (WebMCP, Gemini Omni, Antigravity, Gemini Flash/Omni, Ask YouTube, Search Agents, etc.). Treat tools as backend capability. The client buys speed, more consistent sales execution, better training, less chaos, better follow-up, and measurable business outcomes.

## Differentiation when Google/OpenAI release stronger agent tools

When the user asks how new market releases affect the offer, separate the answer into:

1. **What helps us** — faster operational agents, multimodal video/audio analysis, searchable video/training libraries, background research/reporting agents, web automation, and subagent orchestration.
2. **What does not differentiate us** — saying “we use Gemini,” “we build agents,” “we automate with Google,” “we create AI apps,” “we use WebMCP,” or “we make AI videos.” Everyone will say this.
3. **What makes us generic** — selling “AI solutions,” “custom chatbots,” “automation with AI,” or “apps of AI” without a commercial methodology, implementation plan, team training, or measurement.
4. **What remains our edge** — commercial strategy + methodology + personalized agents + implementation + training + measurement + continuous optimization.

Use this client-facing contrast:

> Google pone los motores. Nosotros construimos el vehículo de ventas que la empresa realmente necesita.

Preferred objection answer when a client says “we can do this with Google/ChatGPT ourselves”:

> Sí, las herramientas están disponibles. Igual que también existen cámaras, CRM, editores de video y plataformas de anuncios. La diferencia no está en tener acceso a la herramienta. Está en saber qué construir, cómo integrarlo a tu proceso comercial, cómo entrenar a tu equipo para usarlo y cómo medir si realmente está generando resultados. Nosotros no vendemos acceso a IA. Construimos un sistema operativo de IA para que tu equipo venda, entrene y dé seguimiento mejor todos los días.
