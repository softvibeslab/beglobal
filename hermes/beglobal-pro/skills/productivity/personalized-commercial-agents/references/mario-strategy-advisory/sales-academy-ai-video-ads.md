# Sales Academy AI — Mario Video Ad Prompts

Use this reference when creating Mario Villanueva video ads similar to the Chris G avatar prompts, especially for Sales Academy AI / AI Department offers.

## Strategic ad pattern

For 45–60 second ads, prefer **4 clips of ~15 seconds** that can be generated separately and assembled in Premiere:

1. **Hook / pain** — human, simple, non-technical.
2. **Story / problem** — show the operational consequence.
3. **Solution** — introduce Sales Academy AI as a system, not a tool.
4. **Offer / CTA** — demo personalized to the client's company.

User liked simple language “como si hablaras con un niño de 10 años,” but still premium/professional. Avoid over-technical scripts in the avatar voice.

## Winning hook line

> Tu equipo de ventas no necesita más presión. Necesita más apoyo.

Why it works:
- does not blame sellers
- creates empathy
- positions the offer as support/training, not replacement
- avoids generic “AI tools” language

## HeyGen prompt skeleton per clip

Keep the same identity rules as Chris G prompts, but adapt to Mario:

- Use HeyGen avatar “Mario Villanueva” exactly as provided.
- Maintain facial identity consistency across all frames.
- Do not regenerate, stylize, reinterpret, or alter the avatar.
- Mario wears: navy tailored suit, black “SER VENDE” shirt, black glasses, brown dress shoes.
- Strictly no tie, no facial hair, no beard, no mustache, no stubble, no tourist clothing.
- Environment: luxurious modern executive resort suite with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the Caribbean Sea.
- Style: premium SaaS commercial, luxury executive cinematic, high-end sales leadership, personal brand authority.
- Lighting: soft natural daylight, warm golden reflections, cinematic contrast, realistic skin lighting.
- Camera: slow push-in or subtle cinematic orbit, shallow depth of field, stable framing, no shake.
- Performance: calm executive confidence, mentor-like authority, emotionally intelligent tone, natural pacing.

## Pitfalls

- Do not lead with Google/WebMCP/Gemini/Antigravity in the script. Those are backend capabilities, not the customer-facing value.
- Do not present the offer as a chatbot or generic AI tool.
- Do not blame sellers; frame the pain as lack of system/support.
- Do not over-explain. For review, first give only the pure script or second-by-second structure if requested.
