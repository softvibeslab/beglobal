# Mario / Chris / Roger commercial pillars

Use this as durable context when answering briefs, sales scripts, video strategy, offer positioning, or go-to-market materials for Mario/BEAT/Chris commercial work.

## The 3 commercial lines

1. **AI production + personalized agents**
   - Treat AI video/content production and personalized AI agents as one commercial line, not two separate offers.
   - Framing: “agentes con súper poderes” and assisted workflows that help clients create, respond, sell, organize, and operate faster.
   - Typical ladder: free 48h demo → paid activation/installation → workflow/community accompaniment.

2. **Mario Villanueva personal brand + BEAT / Del Ser al Vender**
   - Products and ecosystem tied to Mario’s personal brand: Del Ser al Vender, BEAT, BEAT Express, Mentoría BEAT, courses, community, experiences, and content.
   - Positioning: selling from identity, authenticity, clarity, energy, purpose, and strategy — not manipulative or aggressive sales tactics.
   - Audience: entrepreneurs, sellers, commercial leaders, coaches, consultants, and people who want to sell better without losing their essence.

3. **White-label Sales Academy / sales success**
   - For sales leaders in resorts, vacation clubs, and organizations with rep teams that must master a specific sales process.
   - Core problem: training does not scale when it depends only on managers, top producers, or in-person sessions.
   - Differentiator: a Smart Agent trained on company process, culture, systems, objections, language, and top-producer knowledge.
   - Core line: “Sales Academy convierte el conocimiento más valioso de la empresa en una academia inteligente que entrena, guía y mide a los reps todos los días.”
   - CTA: 30-minute diagnostic/demo.
   - Avoid: “Vacation Sales Academy,” using “comercial” for reps/system, generic app framing, more-leads promise, blaming reps, facial hair, blazers/sacos, captions unless requested, unrealistic automatic results.

## Brief-answer pattern

When the user asks to answer a strategic video brief for any of the pillars, answer directly in the brief’s same question structure. Keep it concise, practical, and ready to paste.

Typical questions:
1. ¿Qué vendemos y a quién?
2. ¿Cuál es el problema principal que queremos atacar?
3. ¿Qué resultado quiere lograr el cliente?
4. ¿Qué hace diferente nuestra solución?
5. ¿Qué acción queremos que tome después de ver el video?
6. Opcional: ¿qué NO debemos decir o mostrar?

## Pitfall from session

Do not split “producción con IA” and “creación de agentes personalizados” into two separate proposals when summarizing the commercial portfolio. For this project, they are one pillar. The missing third pillar is Mario’s personal brand products: Del Ser al Vender, BEAT, and Mentoría BEAT.
