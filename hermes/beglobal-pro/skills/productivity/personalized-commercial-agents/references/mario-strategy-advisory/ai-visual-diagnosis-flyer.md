# AI Visual Diagnosis Flyer / Demo Angle

Use this reference when Mario/Chris/Roger want a quick commercial demo of personalized agent capabilities using flyers, posts, ads, landing pages, or visual assets.

## Core demo promise

**“Mándame cualquier flyer, anuncio, landing o post… y te digo en segundos si está vendiendo o solo se ve bonito.”**

Position this as **commercial judgment available 24/7**, not “AI as a toy.” The wow moment is fast, practical feedback that a prospect immediately understands.

## What the agent should analyze

- Whether the piece sells or only looks attractive.
- Whether the message is understood in 3 seconds.
- Hierarchy: headline, offer, dates, price, CTA, logo/brand.
- Legibility on mobile/social feeds.
- Whether the offer is concrete or vague.
- CTA strength and next action clarity.
- Specific copy/design changes to improve conversion.

## Paste-ready flyer copy

Headline:

**¿Tu diseño vende o solo se ve bonito?**

Body:

**Mándame tu flyer, anuncio, post o landing**  
y te digo en segundos:

- si el mensaje se entiende rápido
- si el CTA está débil
- qué cambiaría para vender más

CTA:

**Pide tu diagnóstico visual**  
**Escríbeme: “revisa mi diseño”**

Support line:

**IA + criterio comercial 24/7 para mejorar tus ventas**

Top label option:

**Agentes con súper poderes**

## Design guidance

- Make the headline large and readable first; avoid tiny explanatory text.
- Use high-contrast colors and a clean card for the service explanation.
- Keep copy minimal and action-oriented.
- Prioritize CTA over decorative branding.
- Verify generated flyer images visually before delivery; watch for clipped text in top pills/badges and oversized labels.

## Sales bridge after the demo

After showing the diagnosis, bridge to paid services:

- “También puedo corregir el copy.”
- “Puedo darte 3 versiones mejores.”
- “Puedo adaptar el anuncio para Instagram, WhatsApp o landing.”
- “Puedo crear el guion de venta o seguimiento.”
- “Puedo guardar el estilo de tu marca y revisar futuras piezas igual.”

## Strategic framing

This belongs under the commercial line **AI production + personalized agents (“agentes con súper poderes”)**. It is a low-friction demo/entry offer that makes the agent tangible before selling larger implementation, activation, or accompaniment packages.
